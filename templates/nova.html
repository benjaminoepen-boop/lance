Have you bid in a round before?

If this is not your first semester at Nova SBE, you already took part in a bidding round. Nova's platform still shows you every bid that was placed in your sections back then, in the tab Rank. Those rank lists are the only record of how many bidding points a place in a course has needed in past rounds, and that history is what Lance's analysis of your bids is built on.

First semester at Nova SBE? Then there are no rank lists of yours yet. Come back after this round and share them, so that the next students know how many points a place needed.
