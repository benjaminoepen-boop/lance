How a seat is won

Inside each section the bids are sorted from high to low until the seats are gone. On a tie, whoever has passed more ECTS goes first; after that it is chance.

{{ quote("The algorithm awards seats to students by processing all bid points submitted in descending order (from the most points to the least points given) up until class capacity is maxed at 100%.", "How to Use the Bidding Platform") }}
What you pay

You bid high to win, but you pay the middle between your bid and the lowest winning bid. A bid of 164 against a lowest winning bid of 100 costs (164 + 100) / 2 = 132.

{{ quote("Bidding - primary list: the average between the points allocated to the bid and those given by the last student who successfully gets a seat.", "Bidding Rules") }}

Checked on two real accounts: the lowest winning bid counts across all sections of the course, not only your own. Nova's page does not say so.

On the alternate list you pay your bid, at most what is left. In add/drop you pay your full bid.

{{ quote("Bidding - alternative list: the smaller number between the points allocated to the bid and the remaining points in the student's balance. Add/drop cycle: the actual points allocated to the bid.", "Bidding Rules") }}
Empty is not zero

No bid means you do not want that section. A bid of 0 means you want it but spend no points. Zero bids do win seats when a section has room.

{{ quote("A section with no bid means that the student is not interested in that section, which is different from indicating a bid of zero points.", "Bidding Rules") }}
Clashes are on you

The platform stops overlapping exams. It lets overlapping classes through and does not look at midterms at all. Lance checks all three, date by date.

{{ quote("The system will not block enrollments in courses that have overlapping schedules.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("The platform does not show clashes regarding midterms.", "Bidding Overview") }}
Points

Your points are held as soon as you bid, but for several sections of a course only the highest bid counts. What you do not spend moves to add/drop and is gone when the semester ends, so saving points for later has no value.

{{ quote("When a course or module offers more than one section in the same term, your balance will only consider the highest bid made and subtract those bid points from your overall balance.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("Points cannot be saved or carried over from semester to semester (or over Academic Years).", "Bidding Rules") }}
The alternate list

It only counts after the primary list and it does not hold points while you bid. Fill it. Its order does not matter, and linking a course to a primary course is risky.

{{ quote("Under the alternate list, students choose the elective courses or modules desired as a precaution in case they are not allocated to one or more of the preferences listed in the primary list. This is strongly recommended.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("Linking may be risky because if no slots are available (and because this procedure implies a one-to-one relationship), the bidding platform will not consider your other options in your alternate list.", "How to Use the Bidding Platform") }}

Seen in the data, not in the rules

On rank lists, part of the students with 45 or more ECTS passed rank with their bid × 1.1. Nova's pages do not mention it and we do not know exactly who gets it. Their price is still worked out from the plain bid.
