How is a seat won?

In each section, bids go from high to low until the seats are gone. On a tie, more passed ECTS go first, then chance.

{% call wording() %}{{ quote("The algorithm awards seats to students by processing all bid points submitted in descending order (from the most points to the least points given) up until class capacity is maxed at 100%.", "How to Use the Bidding Platform") }}{% endcall %}
Another section or the alternate list?

Another section is the same course, tried if your first section is full. The alternate list is other courses, tried if a primary course gets no seat.

Does the order count?

On the primary list yes, higher up goes first, but only if you would get a seat in more than one course. On the alternate list no.

This preference is only considered if a student gets a slot in more than one course or module.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("The order in which they are displayed is considered; those placed higher up are given greater priority; The order in which the sections are displayed is considered; sections placed further to the left are given greater priority.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("The order in which choices are listed is not important in the alternate list.", "Bidding Rules") }} {% endcall %}
What is the alternate list for?

Your safety net. It only counts after the primary list and holds no points while you bid. Fill it.

{% call wording() %} {{ quote("Under the alternate list, students choose the elective courses or modules desired as a precaution in case they are not allocated to one or more of the preferences listed in the primary list. This is strongly recommended.", "Bidding Rules") }} {% endcall %}
Is an empty bid the same as 0?

No. Empty means you do not want the section. 0 means you want it but spend nothing, and it can win a seat when there is room.

{% call wording() %}{{ quote("A section with no bid means that the student is not interested in that section, which is different from indicating a bid of zero points.", "Bidding Rules") }}{% endcall %}
Does the platform catch clashes?

Only between exams. Lance also checks classes and midterms, date by date.

{% call wording() %} {{ quote("The system will not block enrollments in courses that have overlapping schedules.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("The platform does not show clashes regarding midterms.", "Bidding Overview") }} {% endcall %}
How many ECTS can I take?

31.5 at most in mandatory and elective courses, 28 recommended. A course on the alternate list does not count.

More questions
Can I bid on several sections of a course?

Yes, and only the highest bid is held. Points you do not spend are gone when the semester ends.

{% call wording() %} {{ quote("When a course or module offers more than one section in the same term, your balance will only consider the highest bid made and subtract those bid points from your overall balance.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("Points cannot be saved or carried over from semester to semester (or over Academic Years).", "Bidding Rules") }} {% endcall %}
And on the alternate list, or in add/drop?

On the alternate list you pay your bid, at most what is left. In add/drop you pay your full bid.

{% call wording() %} {{ quote("Bidding - primary list: the average between the points allocated to the bid and those given by the last student who successfully gets a seat.", "Bidding Rules") }} {{ quote("Bidding - alternative list: the smaller number between the points allocated to the bid and the remaining points in the student's balance. Add/drop cycle: the actual points allocated to the bid.", "Bidding Rules") }}

Checked on two real accounts: the lowest winning bid counts across all sections of the course, not only your own. Nova's page does not say so.

What is the rank bonus?

On rank lists, part of the students with 45 or more ECTS passed rank with their bid × 1.1. Nova's pages do not mention it and we do not know exactly who gets it. Their price is still worked out from the plain bid.
