# First team feature: the week and the bids

## Why

A student should see every clash before bidding and know what a bid will cost, without opening four systems.

## What

Search the courses of the student's programme. Add and remove sections; show what a section would clash with before it is added (LN01 to LN03, LN R1, LN R2). A bid per section with the cut-off of the last round, a suggestion and the expected price (LN04, LN05, LN R3 to LN R7, LN R10). Everything saved without an account (LN11).

## Out of scope

Placing bids on the platform. Logging in to Nova's systems. Modules. Spring timetable until Nova publishes it.

## Done when

- With the bids of a real account, the app shows a seat in Programming TXB for 132 and 33 points left, as the platform charged.
- A refused bid (above the balance) is explained and the saved bid stays.
- After stopping and restarting the app, the plan, the bids and the order are back.
