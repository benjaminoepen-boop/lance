# Can someone use it without help?

## Fresh agents, 7 November 2026

Three agents who had not seen the code or the spec used the app in their own browsers, clicking only what they could see. They thought aloud and reported where they hesitated.

| Who | Device | Task | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| First bidding, Finance | Laptop 1440 × 900 | From nothing to a list for the platform | Done alone, about 38 clicks |
| In a hurry, Management | iPhone 390 × 664 | Do two courses fit my week, what should I bid? | Done alone, first answer after 9 taps |
| Trying to break it | Laptop 1280 × 720 | Example, clash, undo, reload, back button, spring and back | No crash, about 220 clicks |

All three could then say, without looking: nothing has been sent to Nova; a bid of 164 against a lowest winning bid of 100 costs about 132; another section is the same course, the alternate list is other courses.

What they found, and what changed because of it:

- Back from a second course led to the first one. Now it always leads to the list.
- A double click on plus could add the wrong course or cause a server error. Now it adds once.
- Hiding the closed round until the last page came as a shock. Now the first page says it.
- A course in the plan stopped saying that it clashes. Now the row and the bids card say so.
- `Left` and "33 left" meant different things. Now it is `Left` and "33 come back".
- "42 bids of 0" read like an error. Now it reads "42 people bid 0".
- "Seat from 120" next to "Lowest winning bid 111" had no explanation. Now one sentence under *See all bids* explains the difference.
- The order of the lists was explained nowhere. Now the primary list says it, the rules quote Nova, and the alternate list has no numbers.
- Start over also emptied the other semester. Now only the one on show.
- Changing the programme left the old mandatory courses without a bid field. Now each course follows its new status.

Still open: for most courses we have no past price, so the answer to "what should I bid?" is only a range. That needs more rank lists, not more design. The "price looks wrong" report (`you'd pay about 65` for a bid of 90 on a section whose last seat went for 83) is not a bug: Nova uses the lowest winning bid of the whole course, checked on two real accounts (A1), and the app now says so.
